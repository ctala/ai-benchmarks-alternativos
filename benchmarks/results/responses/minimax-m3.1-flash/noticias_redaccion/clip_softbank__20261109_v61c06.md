## What Clip is

Clip was founded by Adolfo Babatz, who remains the company’s chief executive. Its first product was a card reader that connects to a phone and allows any business to accept debit or credit card payments without hiring a traditional bank terminal.

Over time, the company added its own terminals, remote payments through payment links and other financial services for merchants. This evolution took it from a single payment device into a broader financial platform for small businesses.

Its target customers are small and medium-sized businesses, including neighborhood stores, clinics, workshops, restaurants and independent professionals. These are precisely the types of businesses that have often had less access to financial products designed around their needs.

## The problem Clip identified

An important part of payments in Mexico is still made in cash. At the same time, many merchants did not accept cards because of the costs and requirements imposed by banks.

According to Clip, the idea emerged after observing that small businesses were losing sales because they could not accept cards while their customers were gradually carrying less cash. The initial response combined three elements: an affordable device, a straightforward application and registration that could be completed without visiting a branch.

This approach attempted to remove the barriers that kept merchants outside digital payments. Instead of requiring a banking relationship, a traditional terminal and a more complex onboarding process, Clip offered an easier way to begin accepting cards.

## A model without monthly rents or long-term contracts

Clip’s business model is based on charging a commission for each processed sale, without monthly fees or minimum-term contracts. For a small merchant, this means that the cost grows only when sales grow.

The company sells its readers and terminals through physical and online stores. That distribution strategy allowed Clip to reach businesses that had never had a relationship with a bank.

The combination of a low-cost device, simple registration and sales-based pricing is central to Clip’s proposition. It does not require the merchant to take on a fixed infrastructure cost before beginning to receive card payments.

The model also creates a direct connection between the merchant’s activity and the provider’s revenue. Every transaction processed through Clip generates a commission, while merchants can use the service without committing to a monthly rental or a long-term contract.

For merchants, accepting cards offers several clear benefits.
