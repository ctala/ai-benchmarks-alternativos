# Nebius despliega 200.000 GPU Blackwell y casi triplica su capacidad de inferencia

## Entradilla

La compañía neerlandesa Nebius anunció la instalación de 200.000 procesadores gráficos de NVIDIA Blackwell en tres nuevos centros de datos, ubicados en Finlandia, Israel y Kansas City, en Estados Unidos. La expansión requiere una inversión de capital de 3.200 millones de dólares y casi triplicará la capacidad de la compañía para ejecutar modelos de inteligencia artificial, de acuerdo con la empresa. El anuncio llega cuando Nebius reporta un ritmo de ingresos anualizado de 412 millones de dólares, frente a 170 millones un año antes, mientras el 60% de la nueva capacidad ya está contratado en acuerdos plurianuales con clientes empresariales.

La nueva infraestructura está orientada a resolver uno de los principales problemas de la adopting empresarial: la scarcity of capacity to run models in production. CEO Arkady Volozh señaló que la expansión atiende “el cuello de botella de inferencia que estrangula las cargas agentic en producción”.

En términos técnicos, la inferencia es el proceso mediante el cual un modelo de inteligencia artificial ya entrenado recibe una solicitud y genera una respuesta. Las cargas denominadas “agentic” implican sistemas capaces de realizar tareas de varios pasos, como consultar información, utilizar herramientas, tomar decisiones y ejecutar acciones con cierto grado de autonomía.

Para este tipo de aplicaciones, la demanda de cómputo puede aumentar rápidamente conforme más empresas incorporate assistants and automated workflows into their operations. Nebius sostiene que contar con una mayor cantidad de GPU Blackwell permitirá procesar más solicitudes de manera simultánea and reduce bottlenecks in AI service delivery.

## Tres centros para ampliar la capacidad global

Los centros de datos se distributing across Finland, Israel, Kansas City, United States, and this geographic dispersion is part of Nebius's strategy to expand its AI infrastructure. The company did not detail the individual contribution of each site to the total deployment, nor did it provide a breakdown of the investment by location.

One of the facilities will be located in Finland and will operate with hydroelectric power. According to Nebius, the choice is part of its effort to support large-scale computing with lower-carbon electricity sources. The company did not make the same claim about the energy supply of the other two centers.

The announcement also reflects the strategic importance of data center location. AI infrastructure depends not only on the availability of processors, but also on energy, connectivity, cooling, network traffic and the proximity between computing resources and users. For cloud services, a broader distribution of facilities can improve resilience and provide options for routing workloads according to latency, regulatory requirements or customer preferences.

## La demanda empresarial ya está asegurada

Nebius reported an annualized revenue run-rate of 412 million dollars, up from 170 million dollars a year earlier. This represents an increase of 242 million dollars, or 142%, in the pace of revenue reported by the company.

The figure is not the same as recognized annual revenue or a guarantee of future income. An annualized revenue run-rate projects current operating conditions over a twelve-month period, so its subsequent evolution will depend on the actual use of the platform and the conversion of contracts into billed services.

In the case of the new capacity, Nebius said 60% is already committed under multi-year agreements with enterprise customers. This provides some visibility over the utilization of the investment and reduces, although it does not eliminate, the risk of building capacity that remains idle.

The data also shows that Nebius is trying to position itself beyond the experimental stage. Its offer is aimed at organizations that need to deploy models and AI agents in production, rather than merely train or test them. Multi-year contracts can create more stable demand, while the new Blackwell infrastructure may allow the company to attract workloads requiring high processing capacity.
